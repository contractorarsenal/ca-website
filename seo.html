Three Ways Customers Find You

Search, ask, and talk to AI.
Same customer, three paths.

A homeowner might type a search into Google, ask a voice assistant a question, or open ChatGPT and just ask. Each path needs a different kind of work behind the scenes.

SEO
"roof repair near me"

They type a search into Google and scroll a list of results.

→
AEO
"who's a good roofer near me?"

They ask a question, and Google or a voice assistant pulls one direct answer.

→
GEO
Asks ChatGPT directly

They open an AI tool and ask, with no search results page involved at all.

SEO

Search Engine Optimization

The traditional work of helping your website show up and rank well in Google's regular search results. This is the foundation everything else is built on top of.

Example searches
"roof repair near me"
"roofing contractor Seattle WA"
What we work on
  • Service pages built around real search terms
  • City and service-area targeting
  • Technical setup: schema, metadata, site speed

What we don't promise: a #1 ranking or a guaranteed spot on page one. No one legitimately can.

AEO

Answer Engine Optimization

Structuring content so voice assistants and "answer engines," like Google's AI Overviews, can pull a direct answer about your business, sometimes without the customer clicking a link at all.

Example questions
"who's a good roofer near me for storm damage?"
"what should I ask a roofer before hiring them?"
What we work on
  • Direct-answer content near the top of key pages
  • FAQ sections written as real question-and-answer pairs
  • Structured data so answer engines can read your page correctly

What we don't promise: that an AI assistant or Google's AI Overview will choose your business as the answer. We don't control that, and no agency does. Read the full guide →

GEO

Generative Engine Optimization

Making sure your business information is accurate and clear enough that generative AI tools like ChatGPT can cite or mention it by name, even completely outside of Google.

Example prompts
"recommend a reliable roofer in Seattle"
"compare local roofing companies for me"
What we work on
  • Clear, factual business info published consistently everywhere
  • Content that reads like a real answer, not vague ad copy
  • Consistent name, address, phone, and service details

What we don't promise: that ChatGPT, Gemini, or any AI tool will recommend your business by name. We do not control what a third-party AI model decides to generate, and neither does anyone else selling this service. Read the full guide →
