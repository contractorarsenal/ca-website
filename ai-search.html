The Problem

The tool can only repeat what it can read.

Most contractor websites were written to look good to a person skimming, not to be read closely by a machine. When the details are thin, an AI tool has nothing solid to work with, so it leans on whoever explained themselves better.

  • One page covering every service. Nothing says clearly that you do the specific job being asked about.
  • No stated service area. "Serving the greater area" does not tell anyone which cities you cover.
  • Slogans instead of answers. "Quality you can trust" answers no question a homeowner actually asks.
  • Details that disagree. A different phone number or business name on your site, your profile, and your listings.
What AI Search Actually Does

It reads your website,
then answers for you.

An AI tool does not know your business. It reads what it can find, then writes an answer in its own words. If your site never says clearly what you do and where, there is nothing for it to read.

  • What you do. A real page for each service, written in plain words.
  • Where you work. The cities and areas you cover, stated outright.
  • That you are real. The same name, phone number, and details everywhere, plus reviews and photos of actual jobs.
  • Answers to common questions. The things homeowners ask before they hire, answered on the page.

Two Terms You Will Hear

AEO and GEO, in plain words.

AEO

Being the answer when they ask

Someone asks a full question and gets one answer back instead of a list, from a voice assistant or the AI summary at the top of Google. Writing your pages so that answer can come from you is called answer engine optimization, or AEO.

Example questions
"who's a good roofer near me for storm damage?"
"what should I ask a roofer before hiring them?"
What we work on
  • Direct-answer content near the top of key pages
  • FAQ sections written as real question-and-answer pairs
  • Structured data so answer engines can read your page correctly

What we don't promise: that an AI assistant or Google's AI Overview will choose your business as the answer. We don't control that, and no agency does. Read the full guide →

GEO

Being mentioned by AI tools

Someone opens an AI tool like ChatGPT and asks it to help them find a contractor. Keeping your business information clear and consistent enough for those tools to mention you is called generative engine optimization, or GEO.

Example prompts
"recommend a reliable roofer in Seattle"
"compare local roofing companies for me"
What we work on
  • Clear, factual business info published consistently everywhere
  • Content that reads like a real answer, not vague ad copy
  • Consistent name, address, phone, and service details

What we don't promise: that ChatGPT, Gemini, or any AI tool will recommend your business by name. We do not control what a third-party AI model decides to generate, and neither does anyone else selling this service. Read the full guide →
