02 Three ways your website gets understood
SEO

Search Engine Optimization

Helping Google understand your website, services, locations, and authority so your business has a better opportunity to appear when people search for your services.

GEO

Generative Engine Optimization

Structuring your website so AI-powered search systems can better understand your company, services, locations, expertise, and information.

AEO

Answer Engine Optimization

Creating and structuring content around the actual questions customers ask so search engines and AI systems can find clear answers from your website.

We optimize for where search is today and where it's going next.

Nobody can guarantee placement in Google or in AI-generated answers. The work is about making your business easier to find, understand, and trust, so it has a better opportunity to be included.
