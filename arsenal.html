The Name

Why Arsenal?

Arsenal 001 pays homage to Contractor Arsenal, which grew from our roots in building websites for tradespeople. The name goes deeper than that.

Keep us in your arsenal. That's what we tell every contractor we work with. Have the right people, tools, and connections on standby, ready when you need them.

Keep each other in your arsenal. Arsenal 001 is built on the same idea: a curated network of operators you can call, refer, collaborate with, or lean on.

001 means first. This is the founding cohort. The inner circle. The ones who got in before it became known.
